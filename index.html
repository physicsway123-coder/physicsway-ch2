The lab bench

Choose an experiment

Every lab opens in your phone's browser. Works on Android and iPhone, with full controls and sound.

Three steps

From the page to your desk

STEP 1

Scan the QR

Open your phone camera and scan the QR code printed in Mr. Islam's book, or tap a lab above.

STEP 2

Aim at the page

Tap Show on your table and point the camera at the printed poster. The lab locks onto the paper.

STEP 3

Run the experiment

Press Next to follow Mr. Islam step by step, or move the sliders to change current, field and angle.

Show on your table

Locks the lab onto the printed poster. Same experience on every phone.

Place anywhere

Drop the model on any surface around you. iPhone opens it in AR Quick Look.
